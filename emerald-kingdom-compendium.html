Beyond the Desert

Oz is a land of impossible landscapes, ancient magic, and curious peoples. Hidden beyond the shifting sands of the Deadly Desert, the kingdom exists apart from the ordinary world, where golden roads wind through enchanted forests and cities of brilliant color rise from the countryside.

Travelers who arrive in Oz often discover that the land does not follow the rules they know. A field may whisper warnings to those who cross it. A forest may remember every traveler who has passed beneath its branches.

A simple journey from one village to another can become an adventure filled with strange companions, unexpected dangers, and wonders found nowhere else.

Roads Less Traveled

Not every important place in Oz belongs to a country or appears on an official road. Hidden valleys, forgotten castles, talking forests, isolated farms, and impossible landmarks can exist wherever the story requires them.

When adding your own location, give it one memorable rule. Perhaps nobody can lie there. Perhaps every door opens somewhere different. Perhaps the inhabitants have forgotten the sun. A single strange idea can make an otherwise ordinary place feel unmistakably Ozian. Let the map grow as the characters do. Make the rule matter.
