about MEATWATERTM

With as little as 25 calories per 20oz, meatwaterTM is a flavored water beverage designed to support everyday nutrition while flooding your taste buds with overwhelmingly savorly flavorly meat.

With a wide range of flavors and zero-calorie options, meatwaterTM gives consumers the power to tackle life's biggest challenges while also reminding Mother Nature where the fuck you live on the food chain.

what is meatwaterTM

More like what isn't it? It's not vegan. It's not FDA-approved. It's not for herbivores. It's not permissible on an airplane in most countries. But more than that, it's not boring. Plus, it's made with real meat and we think that's just lovely.
